# Message to mentors — deck flow (2026-09-15)

Hi both, attaching the current draft of the hayaData deck. You know the main idea already, so here's just how it flows, so the slides make sense on their own.

**Opening (slides 1-3).** The 80% line as the hook, a one-line intro, a one-sentence agenda.

**The broken flow (slide 4 + first mock demo).** A Superstore dashboard, filtered to Central, profit goes negative. We watch the follow-up question leave the page twice: to an analyst over Slack, and to a standalone chat agent that has to ask which report, which filters, which definition.

**Thesis (slide 5).** Stated only after the room has just lived it.

**The two exits (slide 6).** The analyst costs time. The agent costs context, and the human re-supplies it badly.

**"Why not just an agent?" (slides 7-9).** Steelman the skeptic first, then a five-row dashboard-vs-agent comparison. Neither wins outright, they fit together. Landing line: the report is the prompt, so put the agent on the page.

**The fixed flow (slide 10 + second mock demo).** The identical dashboard with one text box added. Same filter, same question, answered inline, one drill-down.

**What it takes (slides 11-14).** The three things you have to build: grounding, trust, permissions. Each with a short production story.

**Closing (slide 15).** Still open. Leading candidate: the analyst's role shifts from being the follow-up queue to defining the ground the agent stands on.

Feedback is most useful on the closing and on whether the 80% hook needs a source. Anything else you see, say it.
